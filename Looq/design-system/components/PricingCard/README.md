# PricingCard

Packages or service levels side by side: `.plans` of `.plan` cards (meta, name, price, `.plan-list`, button). `.is-featured` highlights one option on a `night` ground.

- At most one featured plan, and only when it is genuinely the most common choice.
- Prices in GH₵ with "from", "/month" or "fixed fee" stated, plus a `.fine-print` line on VAT and official fees.
- Each button preselects its service in the form with `data-service`.
- Published prices are rare on Ghanaian business sites; show them whenever the business can honour them.
