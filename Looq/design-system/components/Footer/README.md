# Footer

The last stop for contact and legal details: `<footer class="site-footer">` on the `night` ground with a `.footer-grid` (brand + short line + CTA, contact, links) and a `.footer-bottom` (copyright, registration number, privacy).

- Repeat name, phone, WhatsApp, email and area; these must match the contact section.
- Include the privacy notice link and the business registration number (charities: registration number; regulated firms: licence).
- Social links only for active, official profiles (`.social` icon buttons with an `aria-label`).
- `.framed` gives the inset, rounded footer for templates with a `frame` value.
