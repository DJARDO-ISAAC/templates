# Ghana conventions

Looq templates are for real Ghanaian businesses. These conventions apply to every template unless the business clearly serves another market.

## Contact

- **Phone:** show local format (`024 555 0187`, `030 295 0418`), link international: `<a href="tel:+233245550187">`.
- **WhatsApp:** a main contact route, often the first one people use. Link `https://wa.me/233245550187?text=Hello%2C%20I%27d%20like%20to…` with a short prefilled message, and only if someone actively monitors it.
- **Forms:** make "Phone or WhatsApp" required and email optional; offer "How should we reply?" with WhatsApp first. Accept international numbers for Ghanaians abroad.
- **Address:** street and area, city, the Ghana Post GPS digital address (`GA-019-1234`) and a landmark ("opposite the Danquah Circle fuel station").
- **Response promise:** "We reply within one working day" works well, provided the business keeps it.

## Money

- Write prices as `GH₵ 1,200` (cedi sign, a space, comma thousands). Use "From GH₵ …", "/month" or "fixed fee" honestly.
- Say whether VAT and official fees (ORC, GRA, permits) are included.
- Name payment methods: mobile money (MoMo), bank transfer, card where offered. For donations and tithes, show MoMo numbers and bank details clearly.
- Published prices are rare on Ghanaian business sites; showing them is a differentiator when the business can honour them.

## Time and dates

- Ghana uses GMT all year (`Africa/Accra`, no daylight saving). Write times as `8:00–17:00` and dates as `13 November 2026`.
- List public-holiday hours or closures.

## Privacy

- Every site that collects personal data needs a privacy notice under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843): who runs the site and how to contact them, what is collected and why, how long it is kept, that it is not sold, and how to ask to see, correct or delete it.
- Data controllers are expected to register with the Data Protection Commission; check the business's status.
- Cookie banners are only needed when tracking cookies are used; if none are, say so.

## Credentials by industry

Show only what the business genuinely holds, link to the official register where one exists, and confirm current rules before launch:

- **All registered businesses:** Office of the Registrar of Companies (ORC) registration and number; GRA TIN where relevant.
- **Accounting:** Institute of Chartered Accountants, Ghana (ICAG) membership and firm licence.
- **Law:** Ghana Bar Association / General Legal Council standing. Lawyer advertising is restricted; keep claims factual.
- **Foreign-owned businesses:** Ghana Investment Promotion Centre (GIPC) registration.
- **Construction:** contractor classification with the Ministry of Works and Housing; professional body membership.
- **Real estate:** licensing under the Real Estate Agency Act, 2020 (Act 1047) and the Real Estate Agency Council.
- **Schools:** Ghana Education Service registration and National Schools Inspectorate Authority (NaSIA) licensing.
- **Nonprofits:** registration as a company limited by guarantee and with the Department of Social Welfare; registration number in the footer.
- **Food businesses:** Food and Drugs Authority (FDA) and Ghana Tourism Authority licences where held.

## Imagery and language

- Use photos of the real business, team and customers wherever possible; stock photos should show Ghanaian or West African people and places.
- Plain, warm English. Local names, towns and dishes, spelled correctly (Kumasi, Tema, kenkey, kontomire).
