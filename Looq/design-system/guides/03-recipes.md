# Section recipes

Starting orders for a home page, per industry and archetype. They are recipes, not rules: a template may reorder, merge or restyle sections. Every page still ends with a contact route and the footer. Components are named in brackets.

## Archetype A (brochure), any industry

- Header (Header) → Hero with the offer, who it is for and where (Hero) → Services overview (SectionHeading + ServiceCard) → Why choose us, with facts (ProcessSteps or FeatureList) → Proof: work, credentials, team or reviews (Testimonial, CredentialBadges, TeamCard) → Short about → FAQ (Accordion) → Contact (ContactDetails) → Footer.

## Archetype B (lead generation): add the lead path

- Primary CTA in the header and hero; the same CTA repeated after services, proof and FAQ.
- Services with prices or "from" prices where possible (ServiceCard, PricingCard), each preselecting the form (`data-service`).
- "What happens next" near the form (ProcessSteps).
- The form section (EnquiryForm) with contact details beside it (ContactDetails) and a success state.
- Mobile shortcuts (ActionBar or the WhatsApp button) and a privacy notice (Dialog or page).

## Restaurants

- **Chop bar / local restaurant (A or B):** bleed hero with food photo and "Order on WhatsApp" → today's specials → menu grid with photos and GH₵ prices (Menu, grid) → order routes (WhatsApp, delivery apps, pickup) → location, hours and open-now (ContactDetails) → reviews → catering and events enquiry (EnquiryForm) → footer.
- **Fine dining (A):** quiet centred or bleed hero → the experience (chef, room) → tasting or à la carte menu as a list (Menu, list) → private dining → reservations link or enquiry → location and hours → footer.
- **Fast food (B):** deals-first hero with "Order now" → best-sellers grid (Menu, grid) → combos and prices (PricingCard) → order routes → branches and hours → footer.
- **Café (A):** warm split hero → menu highlights (Menu, list) → space and atmosphere gallery → events or work-friendly notes → location, hours and open-now → footer.

## Professional services and consulting

- Split hero with a real person and a free consultation CTA → "Sound familiar?" problems → services with prices (ServiceCard or PricingCard) → process (ProcessSteps) → results (ResultStat) → testimonials → team and credentials (TeamCard, CredentialBadges) → FAQ → consultation form → footer.

## Home and local services

- Hero with service area, phone and WhatsApp → services (ServiceCard, icon) → areas served → recent jobs gallery → credentials and guarantee → reviews → quote form with area and photos (EnquiryForm) → footer with an ActionBar on mobile.

## Construction and design

- Bleed hero with a finished project → project portfolio by type (Card grid) → services → process → credentials → testimonials → project questionnaire (EnquiryForm with budget and timeline) → footer.

## Real estate agents

- Hero with "What's my property worth?" → services (sales, letting, management) → areas covered → sold or let examples → agent profiles (TeamCard) → reviews → valuation form → footer.

## Education and training

- Hero with the next intake or open day → programmes → admissions steps (ProcessSteps) → fees and term dates (PricingCard, list) → staff → inspection and accreditation (CredentialBadges) → parent reviews → admissions enquiry → footer.

## Nonprofits and faith

- Hero with mission and a Give/Donate CTA → programmes → impact (ResultStat) → events and service times → ways to give (MoMo, bank, card) → volunteer or join form → leadership and registration (CredentialBadges) → footer.

## Creative and portfolio

- Hero with one strong image or reel → selected work (Card grid, filters) → services and starting prices → process → client quotes → enquiry form with project type and date → footer.
