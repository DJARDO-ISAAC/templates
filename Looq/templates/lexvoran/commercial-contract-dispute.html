The challenge

A business client and a supplier disagreed about what the contract required and when delivery was due. The relationship had value to both sides, and the client wanted to understand its position before taking any formal step.

Our approach

We reviewed the agreement and correspondence, mapped each disputed term against the record, and set out the realistic routes to resolution, from direct negotiation to mediation, with the risks of each explained plainly.

Outcome

Details are withheld to protect client confidentiality.

Key considerations

Clear documentation of scope and delivery terms can narrow a dispute early. Reviewing the record before responding often creates more options.
