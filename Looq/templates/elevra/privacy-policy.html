Privacy Policy

Last update: Oct 8, 2026

1. Overview

Elevra ("we", "us") is a consulting firm advising on strategy, finance, and operations. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We collect only what we need to respond to you and run the site.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Name, email address, company, and role when you book a discovery call or fill out the contact form
  • Details about your business or project that you choose to share in your message
  • Your email address if you subscribe to our newsletter

Information collected automatically:

  • Device and browser type, operating system, and screen size
  • Pages viewed, time on site, referring page, and approximate location (city or country level)
  • Cookie and similar-technology data, covered in Section 6

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and schedule discovery calls
  • To prepare for and follow up on conversations with you
  • To send our newsletter or insights, only if you opted in
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations

4. Legal basis (UK/EU visitors)

We process your data based on your consent (newsletter, non-essential cookies), our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and improving the site, and, where relevant, steps taken at your request before entering a contract.

5. Sharing your information

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate the site and our business, under confidentiality obligations:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure
  • Form handling and email delivery
  • Scheduling tools
  • Analytics
  • Professional advisers, or authorities where required by law

If Elevra is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may transfer as part of that process. We'll tell you if that happens.

6. Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies to make the site work and, with your consent, analytics cookies to see which pages are useful. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies in the banner, and change your browser settings at any time to block or delete them. Blocking essential cookies may affect how the site works.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Enquiries and call notes: up to [24] months after our last contact
  • Newsletter data: until you unsubscribe
  • Analytics data: up to [14] months
  • Client records: as long as needed for legal, tax, and contractual purposes

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these, email us at hello@elevra.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process data outside your country. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Third-party links

This site may link to other websites. We're not responsible for their privacy practices, so check their policies before sharing information.

12. Children's privacy

This site is intended for business users and isn't directed at anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be flagged on this page.
