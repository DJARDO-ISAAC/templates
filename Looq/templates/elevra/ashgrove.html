Case Study

Ashgrove

Ashgrove is a regional credit union serving families and small businesses across the Pacific Northwest. Nine branches, 260 people, and sixty years of member trust.

How Ashgrove Doubled Digital Sign-Ups And Cut Member Churn By 38%

Ashgrove had the trust of its members but not their attention online. Younger members were opening accounts elsewhere, and loan applications took so long that some walked away mid-process. Over eight months we listened to members, mapped the full journey from application to approval, and rebuilt it around fewer steps and faster decisions. This case study covers what we found and what changed.

The Business Problem

Loyal branch members, but a slow digital experience

Loyal members, slow experience.

Ashgrove's branches were full of long-standing members, but new account openings had fallen for three straight years. Almost every competitor offered an app that could open an account in minutes, and Ashgrove's took nearly two weeks.

The loan process was the sharpest pain. Applications passed through five teams, each with its own checklist, and members were asked for the same documents twice. Many gave up before a decision was made, and leadership had no data on where.

The Strategic Response

Digital-first member journey with faster decisions

  1. Listen to members

    Interviewed 60 members on where they got stuck.

  2. Map the journey

    Traced every step from application to approval.

  3. Simplify the process

    Cut the loan application from 14 steps down to 6.

  4. Launch digital first

    Piloted online onboarding at three branches first.

  5. Scale and measure

    Rolled out to all nine branches with weekly tracking.

The Business Impact

Measured over the first 90 days after launch

  • 38% Lower member churnFewer members leaving within a year.
  • 2.1x Digital sign-upsOnline openings vs. the year before.
  • 56% Faster approvalsLoan decisions cut from 9 days to 4.
  • 2 Products per memberAverage up from 1.8 at project start.
