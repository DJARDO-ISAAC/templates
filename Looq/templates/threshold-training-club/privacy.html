Privacy policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

This is placeholder text for the Thres template. Replace it with your own policy before you publish.

What we collect

When you book a class we collect your name, your email address and the day you would like to attend. If you become a member we also store your heart-rate data from classes, your maximum heart rate and your attendance.

How we use it

We use your details to manage bookings, set your training zones and tell you about changes to the timetable. We do not sell your data and we do not share your heart-rate records with anyone outside the coaching team.

How long we keep it

Booking details are kept for twelve months. Training data is kept for as long as you are a member and is deleted within thirty days of a request to remove it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by writing to hello@thres.club.
