The ramp test, explained without the jargon

Eight minutes on a bike that tell us where your five zones really are. No, it is not as bad as it sounds.

The formula says your maximum heart rate is 220 minus your age. The formula is wrong for about one person in three, sometimes by fifteen beats. So before your first class we find your real number.

What you actually do

You pedal. Every minute the bike gets slightly harder. You keep going until you cannot hold the cadence, which for most people takes between seven and ten minutes. The last sixty seconds are unpleasant. The rest is fine.

What we get from it

  • Your true maximum heart rate.
  • The point where your breathing changes, which marks your Thres.
  • Five zones that belong to you rather than to an average.

How often

Every eight weeks. As you get fitter your zones move, and training by old numbers is like wearing last year's glasses. The re-test is also the most honest progress report you will ever get.
