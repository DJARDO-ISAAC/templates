With every membership

The things
we never charge for.

No towel fees, no locker fees, no sign-up fee that turns into an admin fee.

  1. 01

    A strap for every class

    Fitted at the door and paired to the wall. Yours to keep after three months.

  2. 02

    The ramp test

    Eight minutes on a bike to find your real maximum. Repeated every eight weeks.

  3. 03

    A coach who knows you

    Twelve people a class, so your coach knows your name and your numbers.

  4. 04

    Your numbers, afterwards

    Time in each zone, average and peak, sent to your phone before you reach the showers.

  5. 05

    Showers and towels

    Proper ones. Lockers, hair dryers and somewhere to charge your phone.

  6. 06

    A pause button

    Two months a year, from your account. Holidays, injuries and babies all count.
