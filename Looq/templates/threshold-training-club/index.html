About the studio

We coach the number on the wall, not the one on the scale.

Thres opened in a tannery warehouse in 2019 with twelve chest straps and one idea: train at the right effort and the rest follows. Six years on, the straps have multiplied. The idea has not changed.

The five zones

One honest number.
Five ways to use it.

Your heart rate tells the truth about how hard you are working. We put it on the wall, read it with you, and coach you to the right zone for the day.

Classes

Six classes.
Every one has a zone.

Easy days stay easy and hard days earn the name. Pick by how you want to feel when you walk out.

  • Zone 3 · Tempo45 min

    Strength circuit

    Barbells, kettlebells and carries across six stations. Steady effort, clean movement, no ego.
  • Zone 2 · Base45 min

    Engine: row and ride

    Long, easy intervals on the rower and the bike. The class that makes every other class feel lighter.
  • Zone 4 · Thres45 min

    Thres intervals

    Three rounds held just under your limit. Hard to love, impossible to argue with.
  • Zone 1 · Recover40 min

    Mobility and breath

    Slow stretching, breath work and time on the floor. Your heart rate drops, and so do your shoulders.
  • Zone 5 · Max30 min

    Max: bag and sled

    Short, loud and over quickly. Sprints, sled pushes and heavy bag rounds with full recovery between.
  • Zone 2 · Base70 min

    Long run club

    Eight easy kilometres along the harbour at a pace you can talk through. Coffee afterwards.
