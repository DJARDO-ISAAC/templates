Classes

Six classes,
five zones.

Easy days stay easy and hard days earn the name. Pick by how you want to feel when you walk out, then see when it runs.

  • Zone 3 · Tempo45 min

    Strength circuit

    Barbells, kettlebells and carries across six stations. Steady effort, clean movement, no ego.
  • Zone 2 · Base45 min

    Engine: row and ride

    Long, easy intervals on the rower and the bike. The class that makes every other class feel lighter.
  • Zone 4 · Thres45 min

    Thres intervals

    Three rounds held just under your limit. Hard to love, impossible to argue with.
  • Zone 1 · Recover40 min

    Mobility and breath

    Slow stretching, breath work and time on the floor. Your heart rate drops, and so do your shoulders.
  • Zone 5 · Max30 min

    Max: bag and sled

    Short, loud and over quickly. Sprints, sled pushes and heavy bag rounds with full recovery between.
  • Zone 2 · Base70 min

    Long run club

    Eight easy kilometres along the harbour at a pace you can talk through. Coffee afterwards.

Which class, when

Five zones,
five jobs.

Each zone trains something different. A good week has a lot of the first two and a little of the last two.

  • Zone 1

    Recover

    50 to 60% of max

    Blood flow without stress. Speeds up recovery and calms the nervous system.

    Mobility and breath

  • Zone 2

    Base

    60 to 70% of max

    Builds the aerobic engine everything else runs on. Most of your week belongs here.

    Engine, Long run club

  • Zone 3

    Tempo

    70 to 80% of max

    Steady, sustainable work. Where strength and stamina meet.

    Strength circuit

  • Zone 4

    Thres

    80 to 90% of max

    Raises the pace you can hold. Hard, and worth it once or twice a week.

    Thres intervals

  • Zone 5

    Max

    90 to 100% of max

    Short, all-out efforts for speed and power. A little goes a long way.

    Max: bag and sled
