How we coach

Three rules
we do not bend.

They are on the wall by the door, in smaller type than the heart rates.

  1. 01

    Easy means easy

    If the plan says Base, we will slow you down. Out loud, if we have to.

  2. 02

    The number decides

    Not how you look, not how the person next to you is doing. Your heart rate, on your scale.

  3. 03

    Nobody trains alone

    Twelve people, one coach, every class. We know your name and your last re-test.

First class

Your first class
is on us.

Come fifteen minutes early. We fit a strap, find your maximum with a short ramp test and show you round. Then you train.
