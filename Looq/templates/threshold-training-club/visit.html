Getting here

Three ways
to the door.

However you arrive, come fifteen minutes early the first time.

  • On foot

    Five minutes from the station

    Leave by the river exit, turn left along the water and keep going until you smell coffee.

  • By bike

    Racks at the door

    Twelve covered spaces outside and a pump on the wall. Bring your own lock.

  • By car

    Park across the bridge

    The public car park on Lime Kiln Road is three minutes away. The first two hours are free before 08:00.
