House shito

Shito is Ghana's black pepper sauce: dried fish, shrimp and chilli fried slowly in oil with ginger and garlic until almost black. A spoon of it improves nearly everything on our menu.

  • 90 mintotal
  • 30 minhands on
  • 12servings

Ingredients

Makes

servings (about 500 ml)

Method

0 of 6 steps done

  1. Blend the onions, garlic and ginger into a smooth paste. Add a splash of water only if the blender sticks.

  2. Heat the oil in a heavy pot on medium. Add the paste and fry, stirring often, until it turns golden and smells sweet.

    20:00

  3. Stir in the tomato paste and fry until it darkens a little.

    05:00

  4. Turn the heat to low. Add the chilli, shrimp and fish and stir well. It will spit, so stand back.

  5. Cook on the lowest heat, stirring every few minutes and scraping the bottom, until the sauce is very dark brown, almost black.

    45:00

  6. Season with salt, take it off the heat and let it cool completely before you jar it. Keep a layer of oil on top.

From our kitchen

  • Low heat and patience are everything. If it burns, the bitterness never leaves.
  • Kept in a clean jar under its oil, shito lasts two months in the fridge.
