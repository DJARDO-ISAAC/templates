Rice & beans

Red red

say “red red”VegGF

Black-eyed beans stewed in red palm oil, with fried ripe plantain and gari.

Make it
From
All of Ghana
Allergens
None. Fish only if you add it.

About the dish

The name comes from the two reds on the plate: beans cooked down in red palm oil with tomato and onion, and plantain fried until orange.

Most cooks add a little smoked fish to the beans. Ours is vegan unless you ask, so add fish or an egg if you want it the way an Accra auntie would make it.

Cook red red at home

Eat it like a local

Sprinkle gari over the beans just before you eat. It soaks up the palm oil and adds crunch.
