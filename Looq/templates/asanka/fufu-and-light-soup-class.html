Class · Saturday 21 November 2026

Fufu & light soup class

Pound fufu in the yard with Efua, make light soup from scratch and eat what you made.

Fufu is a two-person job, and you will do both jobs: pounding, and turning the dough between strokes without losing a finger. Efua will show you how to judge when it is smooth.

While the cassava boils we make light soup from the start: browning the goat, blending the pepper and tomato, and learning when to stop simmering.

What you will eat

  • Fufu, pounded by hand
  • Goat light soup
  • Raw pepper in the asanka

Included

  • All ingredients and an apron
  • Lunch, which is what you made
  • A recipe card to take home
