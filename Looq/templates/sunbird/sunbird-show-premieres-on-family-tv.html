The Sunbird Show Premieres on Family TV

Thirteen episodes, one big adventure

The first season of The Sunbird Show has thirteen short episodes made for children and the grown-ups watching beside them. Each one starts with a question a child might really ask, then heads outside to find the answer.

When to watch

New episodes air on Saturday mornings. Every episode then joins our Shows page a week later, so nobody has to miss one because of a football match or a birthday party.

Learning together

We have also written a short activity sheet for each episode. They use things most homes already have, like paper, string and bottle tops.
