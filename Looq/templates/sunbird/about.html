Welcome
To Sunbird

Sunbird began with a simple gap: too few learning materials showed children the places, animals and stories around them. Plenty of good lessons existed, yet many left children feeling that knowledge lived somewhere else. So we set out to make fun, engaging resources rooted in Africa’s landscapes, wildlife, history and culture.

Our Learning Goals

  • Develop creativity.
  • Build critical thinking skills.
  • Connect subjects, so science, history and art support each other.
  • Make interactive materials that feel familiar and relevant to children.
  • Strengthen reading and comprehension through proven reading methods.

Sunbird Explorers are:

  • Creative.
  • Problem solvers.
  • Observant, and always asking why.
  • Wonderful, and proud of who they are.
