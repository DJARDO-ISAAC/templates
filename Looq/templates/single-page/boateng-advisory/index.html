FAQ

Questions clients ask before we start

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

Should I register a business name or a company?

It depends on your plans. A business name (sole proprietorship) is cheaper and quicker, but you are personally liable for the business’s debts. A limited company protects your personal assets and is usually expected by banks, investors and big clients. We’ll explain both at your consultation and recommend one.

How long does registration take?

Once we have all your documents, most registrations are completed within one to two weeks, depending on the Registrar of Companies. We’ll give you a realistic date in your quote and keep you updated.

My annual returns are overdue. Can you help?

Yes, this is one of the most common jobs we do. We’ll check your company’s record, file the missing returns, tell you exactly what penalties apply, and set up reminders so it doesn’t happen again.

Can you guarantee my loan or grant will be approved?

No, and you should be careful of anyone who does. We make sure your plan is complete, honest and in the format the bank or funder expects, which gives you the best chance. Many of our clients are approved on the first application.

Do you work with businesses outside Accra, or owners abroad?

Yes. We work with clients in Tema, Kumasi, Takoradi, Koforidua and Ho, and with Ghanaians abroad who are starting or running a business at home. Most work can be done on WhatsApp and email, and we visit when it matters.

How do I pay?

By mobile money or bank transfer. One-off projects are 50% to start and 50% on completion. Monthly services are paid at the start of each month. You always get a receipt.

Will you keep my information confidential?

Always. Your documents and figures are only seen by the people working on your job, and we never name clients without their written permission.
