## Content

Boateng Advisory Ltd, its team, clients, figures, reviews, company number and contact details are fictional. The page is a design template.

The services and how they are sold are modelled on real Ghanaian SME consulting firms. Check these facts against current official sources before a client goes live:

- ORC registration fees (2 Feb 2026 schedule: business name GH₵ 130, company limited by shares GH₵ 585): https://orc.gov.gh/forms/FINAL-FEES-AND-CHARGES-2026.pdf
- Annual returns penalties (GH₵ 1,000 for 1–4 years in default, GH₵ 2,000 for 5 years or more): https://orc.gov.gh/annual-returns-2026/
- PAYE due by the 15th of the following month, SSNIT by the 14th
- Consultant price ranges are based on published Ghanaian offers, e.g. business plans at GH₵ 3,000–12,000: https://expandgh.com/advisory/product/get-investor-ready-business-plans-for-1m-in-equity-grants-or-loans/
