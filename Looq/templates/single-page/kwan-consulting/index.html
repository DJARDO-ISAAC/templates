Questions about starting and growing a business in Ghana

Short answers about business setup, market entry, research and support. Ask us anything else at your free consultation.

What does a business consulting firm in Ghana do?

We help you make and carry out business decisions: researching a market, registering and setting up a company, writing a plan, building a brand, organising operations and staff, and managing projects on the ground. You can use us for one task or the whole journey.

Can you help me start a business in Ghana while I live abroad?

Yes, a large share of our clients are Ghanaians abroad. We handle registration, banking introductions, premises, suppliers and hiring locally, send you regular updates on WhatsApp and email, and meet you on site when you visit.

Do foreign investors need anything extra to register?

Usually, yes. Businesses with foreign ownership generally register with the Ghana Investment Promotion Centre (GIPC) as well as the Office of the Registrar of Companies, and capital and permit rules can apply. We confirm the current requirements for your sector and structure before you commit any money.

How long does it take to set up a business in Ghana?

A simple local company can be registered within a few weeks once your documents are ready. Foreign-owned companies, regulated sectors and businesses that need premises, permits or staff take longer. We give you a realistic timeline in your quote.

How much do your services cost?

Single services such as a feasibility study or brand strategy start from GH₵ 2,500, and the Ghana Launch Package starts from GH₵ 12,000. Official fees are charged at cost. You always get a fixed, written quote after the free consultation.

Do you provide market research for SMEs?

Yes. We run market sizing, customer interviews, competitor checks, pricing research and site visits, scaled to your budget, so you can decide with evidence instead of guesswork.

Can you help an existing business grow?

Yes. For established businesses we review strategy, marketing, operations and systems, find what is holding growth back, and help your team put the fixes in place.
