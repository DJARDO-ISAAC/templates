Quality

To spec, or it doesn’t leave the warehouse.

Most grain problems start between the field and the truck. These are the five checks every lot goes through, in this order.

  1. 01

    Dry on the cob

    Farmers dry on tarpaulins, never on bare ground, until our meter reads below 15%.

  2. 02

    Shell and clean

    Machine shelling, then a sieve and air cleaner take out cobs, stones and dust.

  3. 03

    Test the lot

    Moisture, foreign matter, broken grain and an aflatoxin strip test on every lot.

  4. 04

    Bag and tag

    New polypropylene bags, 100 kg net on a checked scale, tagged with the lot number.

  5. 05

    Weigh and load

    Every truck crosses our weighbridge. You get the ticket and lot reports before it leaves.

Questions

Asked at the warehouse gate

Something else? Call us, or send a WhatsApp in English, Dagbani or Twi.

Can I see the grain before I pay?

Yes. We send the lab report for each lot on WhatsApp, and you are welcome to send your own inspector to Tamale. For first orders we can ship a 50 kg sample to your mill.

What happens if a load arrives below spec?

Test it on arrival and tell us within 48 hours. If it doesn’t match the lot report, we replace the bags or refund them, and we pay the transport.

Do you export?

Not directly. We sell to Ghanaian buyers and exporters who hold the export permits. Ask us if you need grain to an export standard such as a lower aflatoxin limit.

Who can join the farmer scheme?

Farmers in our eleven districts with 1 to 20 acres, who join through a farmer group of at least five members. Bring a Ghana Card and a mobile money number. Women farmers and young farmers are welcome; about 40% of our farmers are women.

What if the rain fails and my harvest is poor?

Every farmer in the scheme has weather-index insurance through the Ghana Agricultural Insurance Pool. In a bad year the insurance pays toward your repayment, and we never take more than half of what you harvest.

Can I sell you grain without joining the scheme?

Yes, from October to January. Bring dry, clean grain to the warehouse in Kanvili or a community point. We test it, weigh it and pay by MoMo the same day.
