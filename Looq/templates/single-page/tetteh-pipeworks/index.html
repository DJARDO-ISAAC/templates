When the mains goes off

Water every day, even when the supply doesn't come

Many Tema homes only get mains water on some days. A storage tank, the right pump and a float switch keep your taps running in between, without burning out the pump.

  • Sized for your household: we work out how many litres you use and how long supply gaps usually last.
  • Float switch protection: the pump stops when the tank is empty, so it never runs dry.
  • Clean water: a covered tank, a cleaning schedule and a reminder from us every six months.

Questions

Before you call

Do you come out at night or on Sundays?

For burst pipes and flooding, yes: call 030 330 6142 and we'll send whoever is nearest. Night and Sunday callouts are GH₵ 250.

Do you buy the parts?

Yes, if you want us to. We show you the part and the shop receipt, and you pay exactly what it cost. You can also buy parts yourself and we'll fit them.

Can you fix electrical faults on pumps and heaters?

We handle the plumbing side. For wiring faults we bring in a certified electrician, because electrical work in Ghana must be done by a certified wiring professional.

How do I pay?

Mobile money or cash after the job is done and tested. We give a written receipt with the guarantee on it.
