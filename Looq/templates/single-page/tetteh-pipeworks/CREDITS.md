# Tetteh Pipeworks — credits and launch checklist

Wave 1 template for **Home & local services** (archetypes A brochure + B lead generation): a plumber in Tema.

## Content is fictional
Tetteh Pipeworks, its staff, reviews, prices, phone numbers, email, workshop location and Ghana Post GPS code are made up. A web search (Nov 2026) found no plumbing business with this name; check the Registrar of Companies before using it for a client.

The house water-system drawing, its seven problem areas and the "before we arrive" tips are original. The FAQ line about electrical work reflects Ghana's requirement that wiring is done by certified electrical wiring professionals; confirm the client's own arrangements.

## Before a client goes live
- [ ] Replace name, phone, WhatsApp, email, workshop address and GPS code.
- [ ] Replace every price (callout fee, price table, problem-finder ranges, night/Sunday rate) with the client's real prices.
- [ ] Confirm the promises: same-day visits before 2pm, 30-minute callback, 90-day guarantee.
- [ ] Replace the areas list and the two reviews with real, approved ones.
- [ ] Connect the callback form (`action` + `data-endpoint="ajax"`).
- [ ] Replace placeholder photos with the client's own work.
