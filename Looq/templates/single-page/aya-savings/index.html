Why Aya

A savings account that gets out of your way.

No minimum-balance traps. No teaser rates. No fine print designed to be missed. Just the things a household actually needs from a bank.

No withdrawal penalties.

Reach your money when life happens. No fees, no minimum-balance trap, no quiet charge at the end of the month.

Rates you can plan on.

Published monthly in plain language. No teaser rate that quietly resets after three months.

Goals that keep you honest.

Name what you are saving for, set a date, and watch the balance close the gap. School fees. A shop. A roof.

Borrow against your record.

Your savings history becomes a credit history. A loan should reward the habit, not punish the need.

Terms without the fine print.

Every fee on one page. If we cannot explain a product at a kitchen table, we will not sell it.

A person you can call.

One branch on Oxford Street, one phone line, and a human who knows your name. Not a ticket number.

The passbook

Your money, in plain sight.

Every deposit, every cedi of interest, every goal — in one place. Open the app or walk into the branch; the number is the same.
