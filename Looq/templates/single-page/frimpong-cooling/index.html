FAQ

Good to know before we come.

Do you charge just to come and look?

The inspection visit is GH₵ 150 within Accra and Tema. If you go ahead with the repair, we deduct it from your bill. If we can’t fix the problem, you don’t pay for the repair.

Do you bring the parts?

We carry common parts such as capacitors, breakers, sockets and gas. For anything else we tell you the part and its price first, and buy it only once you agree. You keep the old part.

Are your electricians licensed?

Yes. Our wiring work is done and signed off by Energy Commission certified electrical wiring professionals. Ask to see the licence card when we arrive.

What does the 90-day guarantee cover?

If the same fault comes back within 90 days because of our work, we return and fix it at no charge. Parts carry their maker’s warranty on top.

How do I pay?

After the job, by mobile money or cash. Larger installations and rewiring take a deposit for materials, agreed in writing. You get a receipt for every payment.
