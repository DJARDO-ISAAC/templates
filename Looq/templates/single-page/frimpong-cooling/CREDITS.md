## Content

Frimpong Cooling & Electrical, its staff, reviews, prices, address, phone number and email are fictional; the page is a design template. The phone number and domain are placeholders and must be replaced.

Before a client goes live, check:

- **Licensing.** In Ghana, electrical wiring work must be done by an Energy Commission certified electrical wiring professional. Only keep the "licensed" claims if the client's electricians hold current certificates, and consider showing the certificate numbers.
- **Prices.** The GH₵ prices are realistic for Accra in late 2026 but are examples. Use the client's real price list.
- **Reviews and rating.** Replace the sample reviews and the 4.9 rating with the client's real Google reviews, and point the rating link at their Google Business Profile.
- **Areas.** Edit the area chips, the `datalist` in the page and the `covered` / `outer` lists in `main.js` together.
- **Form.** The quote form only validates in the browser. Connect it to the client's form handler or email service, and make sure the photo upload is supported there.
