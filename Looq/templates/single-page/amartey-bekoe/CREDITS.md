## Content

Amartey Bekoe & Co., its partners, clients, figures, licence number, addresses and contact details are fictional; the page is a design template. The firm name was checked against ICAG's list of licensed firms (November 2026) and does not match a real firm.

The services and the key dates follow how Ghanaian accounting firms work. Confirm the dates and rules with current GRA, SSNIT and ORC guidance before a client goes live:

- SSNIT contributions by the 14th of the following month
- PAYE and withholding tax by the 15th of the following month
- VAT returns by the last working day of the following month
- Corporate tax instalments by the end of each quarter, and the annual return within four months of the year end
- ORC annual returns by the date the ORC sets each year (30 June in 2026)
