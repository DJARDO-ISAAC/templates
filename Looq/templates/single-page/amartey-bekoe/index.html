FAQ

Questions we hear before an engagement.

Something else? Ask a partner directly through the proposal form.

How are your fees set?

We agree a fixed fee for the scope in your engagement letter, based on the size and complexity of your organisation. If the scope changes, we tell you and agree any change in writing before doing the extra work.

We want to change auditors. How does that work?

Your shareholders appoint the new auditor, and we contact your outgoing auditor for professional clearance as ICAG’s code requires. We help with the board and shareholder resolutions, and plan the handover so your timetable doesn’t slip.

When should we start planning our audit?

Before your year end. Planning in October or November for a December year end lets us agree the timetable, review risks early and sign your accounts by the end of March.

Does a small company need a full audit?

Not always. Some small companies can file accounts with a review report instead of a full audit. We check your figures against the current thresholds and tell you which applies.

How do you protect our information?

Client information is confidential under our professional code and our engagement letters. Documents are shared through a secure client folder, and our handling of personal data follows Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).
