The last mile

Money crosses the ocean. Meaning gets lost on the way.

Three things go wrong between a kind intention and a paid caregiver. HomeCare is built around each one.

01

You send. Then you guess.

The transfer lands. Whether it became rent, medicine or a new phone is another matter. Asking feels like distrust.

02

Care work has no paperwork.

A good caregiver can look after a parent for years with no contract, no payslip and no proof of the work she does.

03

Every month starts at zero.

No salary record means no credit history. The people who hold families together cannot borrow for a fridge or a market stall.

What we check before a caregiver is listed

Verification is the product. Every caregiver goes through the same six steps — and again every 12 months.

  1. 01
    Identity

    Ghana Card, face match and a check against the national register.

  2. 02
    References

    Two previous employers contacted by our care team, not by the caregiver.

  3. 03
    Police record

    A criminal-record check issued within the last 90 days.

  4. 04
    Skills interview

    A care lead assesses cooking, hygiene, first aid and communication.

  5. 05
    Home visit

    We visit the caregiver's address and confirm it with a utility bill.

  6. 06
    Contract and account

    Signed terms, a Sankofa salary account and safeguarding training.

If any step fails, the caregiver is not listed. Families can see the status of every check, but never another family's details.
