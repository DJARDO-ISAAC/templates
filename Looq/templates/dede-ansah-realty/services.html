Services and fees

What we do, and exactly what it costs

No surprise charges. Our fees are written here and in your agreement before we start.

For tenants

Renting a home

Tell us your area, budget and move date. We shortlist homes we've visited, arrange viewings, check the tenancy agreement and stay with you until you get the keys.

For landlords

Letting and managing your property

We photograph and list your home, vet tenants, prepare the agreement and, if you want, collect rent and handle repairs while you're away.

Letting fee: 5% of the rent, paid by the landlord

For buyers and sellers

We value your home, market it and handle viewings and offers. Buyers get the title documents checked before any deposit is paid.

For land buyers

Found a plot yourself? We run the six checks on it, visit the site and give you a written report before you pay the seller.

Fixed fee: GH₵ 2,500 per plot

How it works

From first message to keys in hand

The same five steps whether you're renting, buying or handing us your property to let.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Area, budget, number of rooms and when you want to move, by form, call or WhatsApp.

  2. We shortlist and check

    Only homes we've visited, with the advance and documents confirmed.

  3. Viewings, in person or on video

    We show you round, or walk you through on a WhatsApp video call if you're abroad.

  4. Agreement checked before you sign

    We go through the tenancy or sale agreement line by line with you.

  5. Keys, and we stay in touch

    A move-in inventory with photos, and one call to us if something goes wrong.

Questions

Things people ask us

Why don't you list homes that ask for two years' advance?

Because the Rent Act limits advance rent to six months for tenancies longer than six months, and we don't want our tenants paying more than the law allows. Landlords who work with us agree to the limit before we list their home.

Can I rent or buy from abroad?

Yes. We do live viewings on WhatsApp video, send photos of every document, and you only pay once you've seen the agreement. Many of our buyers live in the UK, the US and Germany.

Why are all your prices in cedis?

Rent and prices in Ghana must be quoted and paid in cedis. If you earn in another currency, we'll tell you the cedi amount on the day you pay.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Our agents are licensed by the Real Estate Agency Council under the Real Estate Agency Act, 2020. Ask to see the licence at any viewing.
