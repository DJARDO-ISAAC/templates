# Dede Ansah Realty — credits and launch checklist

Wave 1 template for **Real estate agents** (archetypes A brochure + B lead generation). It shows a short, hand-picked set of properties; a searchable listings database belongs to Wave 2 ("Real estate listings").

## Content is fictional

Dede Ansah Realty, its team, clients, quotes, properties, prices, phone numbers, email, address and Ghana Post GPS code are made up. Web searches (Nov 2026) found no real estate business with this name; check the Registrar of Companies before using it for a real client.

## Facts the copy relies on (checked Nov 2026)

| Claim in the copy | Source |
|---|---|
| Agents must be licensed by the Real Estate Agency Council under the Real Estate Agency Act, 2020 (Act 1047) | ghalii.org/akn/gh/act/2020/1047 ; gna.org.gh (May 2026) |
| Advance rent is capped at six months for tenancies longer than six months (Rent Act, 1963, Act 220, s.25(5)) | ghanaweb.com rent-act explainer ; 2026 Rent Control enforcement reports |
| Rent and prices must be quoted in cedis | Rent Control statements reported by modernghana.com and others |
| Agent commission on rentals is at most 5% of the rent and paid by the landlord, not the tenant | myjoyonline.com ; ghanaweb.com Rent Control reports |
| The six land checks (own surveyor's site plan, Lands Commission search, Customary Land Secretariat, seller's authority, litigation and occupants, indenture, stamp duty and registration) | Lands Commission client charter ; graphic.com.gh ; thebftonline.com |

No licence number is shown: never add one that the client has not given you.

## Before a client goes live

- [ ] Replace the business name, team, phone, WhatsApp, email, address and GPS code.
- [ ] Add the client's real REAC licence details if they want them shown.
- [ ] Replace every property (photos, prices, facts, terms) with the client's own, and update the "updated" date on the Properties page.
- [ ] Confirm the fees table (5% letting, 8% management, 5% sale, GH₵ 2,500 land check) matches the client's real agreement.
- [ ] Replace the two client quotes with real, approved ones.
- [ ] Connect the enquiry form: set its `action` and `data-endpoint="ajax"` (see the shared main.js notes). Until then it shows the demo success message.
- [ ] Re-check the Rent Act wording if the new Rent Bill passes.

## Typefaces

Instrument Serif (headings) and Hanken Grotesk (body), both SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted in `assets/fonts/`.

## Inspiration

Layout research on ThemeForest real estate agent templates (Piller, "Real Estate - Agent & Single Property"). Kept: clear property cards with price, facts and a status badge; a visible process; fees stated up front. Avoided: portal-style search bars, shop and cart pages, and stat counters. No layout, wording or assets were copied.
