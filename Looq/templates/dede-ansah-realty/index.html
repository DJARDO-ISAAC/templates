Buying land

Six checks before you pay for a plot

Land sold twice, or by people with no right to sell it, is one of the most common and costly problems in Ghana. We run these checks on every plot we list, and we'll run them for land you found yourself.

Ask us to check a plot

  1. Your own surveyor draws the site plan

    Not the seller's. We compare it with what's on the ground.

  2. Official search at the Lands Commission

    Who is recorded as owning the land, and whether anyone else has registered an interest.

  3. Search at the Customary Land Secretariat

    For stool, skin or family land, to confirm the allocation.

  4. Confirm the seller's right to sell

    The chief and elders, or the family head and principal members, must agree.

  5. Check for court cases and occupants

    We visit the site and look for disputes, squatters or encroachment.

  6. Indenture, stamp duty and registration

    The sale is signed, stamped and registered at the Lands Commission in your name.
