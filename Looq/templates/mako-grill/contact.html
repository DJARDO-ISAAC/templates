Questions

Asked at the counter

Do I need to book a table?

No. The Osu branch is walk-in only. For groups of 15 or more, call ahead and we will put tables together.

How can I pay?

MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money, Visa and Mastercard, or cash. For WhatsApp orders, pay by MoMo when you confirm.

Where do you deliver?

Through Bolt Food and Glovo, wherever they reach in Accra. Spintex covers the east (Tema, Sakumono, Lashibi); Osu covers the centre and west.

Is the food very spicy?

Only if you want it to be. Every dish can be made mild, medium or hot, and the chillies on the menu show our standard level.

Is there parking?

Osu has street parking on Oxford Street; it is busiest after 19:00 on Fridays. Spintex has six bays in front of the kitchen for pick-ups.

Privacy notice

Mako Grill Ltd runs this website. When you send a catering or contact form, we receive your name, phone number, email if you give it, and the details of your event or question.

We use them only to reply, prepare a quote and deliver your order. We do not sell them or add you to marketing lists. We keep enquiries for 12 months, and records of confirmed orders for as long as Ghana's tax rules require.

Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. Email hello@makogrill.com.gh or call 055 000 0101.

WhatsApp, Bolt Food and Glovo have their own privacy policies for orders you place with them.
