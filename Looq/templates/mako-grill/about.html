From a khebab stand to two grills

Mako means pepper in Twi. It started in 2016 with one charcoal grill on Oxford Street, and we still cook the same way.

Our story

Same coals, more seats

Kojo Asante set up a khebab stand outside an Osu bar in 2016, with a marinade from his mother's kitchen in Koforidua. People queued, then asked for chicken, then for jollof to go with it.

By 2019 the stand had become a 40-seat grill under the mango tree on Oxford Street. In 2023 we opened a takeaway kitchen on Spintex Road for deliveries to the east of the city. Both branches still light hardwood charcoal every morning at nine, and every marinade is made the night before.

We cook to order. That means about 20 minutes for a quarter chicken, and it means it reaches you hot.

Head cook

“Jollof is patience. You let the base fry until the oil comes up red, you cover it, and you leave it alone. That's the smoke people taste.”

Esi Mensah has run the Mako kitchen since 2019. She trained at Accra Technical University and cooked at hotels in Takoradi and Accra before joining us. Every new cook spends their first month on her rice station.

Kitchen standards

What we promise
on every plate

Both kitchens hold a Food and Drugs Authority food hygiene permit, which is on display at the counter.

  • Local and fresh

    Chicken from two farms in the Eastern Region, tilapia from the Volta, vegetables from Agbogbloshie market every morning.

  • Nothing under a lamp

    Food is cooked when you order it. Rice and stews are made in the morning and kept at serving temperature, never reheated the next day.

  • Allergens on the menu

    Every dish lists its allergens. Ask the counter and we will show you the recipe card.

Privacy notice

Mako Grill Ltd runs this website. When you send a catering or contact form, we receive your name, phone number, email if you give it, and the details of your event or question.

We use them only to reply, prepare a quote and deliver your order. We do not sell them or add you to marketing lists. We keep enquiries for 12 months, and records of confirmed orders for as long as Ghana's tax rules require.

Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. Email hello@makogrill.com.gh or call 055 000 0101.
