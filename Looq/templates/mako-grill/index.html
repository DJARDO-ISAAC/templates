    Why it tastes like this

    Slow coals,
    fast service

    Mako means pepper in Twi. We started as a khebab stand on Oxford Street in 2016 and still cook the same way: real hardwood charcoal, marinades made overnight, nothing sitting under a lamp.

    • Chicken from Ghanaian farms. Delivered fresh, never frozen, from two poultry farms in the Eastern Region.

    • Ready in about 20 minutes. Message ahead on WhatsApp and it is wrapped when you arrive.

    • Spice you can choose. Every dish comes mild, medium or hot. Just say.

    Privacy notice

    Mako Grill Ltd runs this website. When you send a catering or contact form, we receive your name, phone number, email if you give it, and the details of your event or question.

    We use them only to reply, prepare a quote and deliver your order. We do not sell them or add you to marketing lists. We keep enquiries for 12 months, and records of confirmed orders for as long as Ghana's tax rules require.

    Your rights

    Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. Email hello@makogrill.com.gh or call 055 000 0101.

    WhatsApp, Bolt Food and Glovo have their own privacy policies for orders you place with them.
