A hot lunch for every pupil in Sena Valley

Twenty-two schools, one simple promise: nobody learns long division on an empty stomach.

School mealsSena Valley

Why this matters

In Sena Valley the lean months run from February to May, and that is exactly when classrooms empty out. Children stay home because there is no reason to walk five kilometers on nothing.

Parents here already know the answer. They formed cooking committees, set up rotas and found farmers ready to sell maize and beans. What they need is the money to buy in bulk and build proper stoves.

Where the money goes

  • Local maize, beans and greens62%

  • Fuel-saving stoves and kitchens21%

  • Cook training and food safety11%

  • Monitoring and reporting6%

Latest updates
