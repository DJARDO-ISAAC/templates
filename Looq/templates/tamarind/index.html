Why Tamarind

Named for the tree where school often begins

Across much of the world, the shade of a big tamarind is where a village meets, where stories get told and, often, where the first class is held. It grows slowly and lives for centuries. That is the kind of work we want to do.

  • Communities choose the work and own it
  • Local staff, four in five living where they work
  • Every result published, including what failed

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers

It is your money, so ask us anything.

All questions
Where does my donation actually go?
In 2025, 87 cents of every dollar went straight to programs, 8 cents to fundraising and 5 cents to administration. You can read the full breakdown on our impact page.
Do you sponsor individual children?
No. We fund whole schools and villages, so no child is singled out and every child benefits. We believe that is both fairer and more effective.
What happens if a project fails?
We tell you. Each year we report on what did not work, including wells that have broken, and on what we changed as a result.
Is my donation tax deductible?
Tamarind is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so gifts are tax deductible to the extent the law allows. Your receipt arrives by email as soon as you give.
