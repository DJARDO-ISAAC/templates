School meals

3.4 million lunches, and rising

Average attendance rose by 18% in the first year at schools where lunch was new. The cost per meal has stayed close to fifty cents.

What did not work

Three things we got wrong

You should hear this from us first.

Six wells stopped working
Four needed parts we could not source locally, so we now keep regional spares. Two committees had stopped collecting fees, and we are working with them again.
A tablet pilot that failed
We tried digital lessons at eight schools. The batteries failed, and the teachers preferred books. We ended it after a year and spent the rest on reading corners.
Garden yields fell short
Our first gardens produced about half of what we forecast, because we planted the wrong crops for the soil. Local agronomists now choose.
