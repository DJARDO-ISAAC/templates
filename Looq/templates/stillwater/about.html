Our story

A dozen people and a borrowed hall.

Stillwater began in 1987, when twelve neighbors started meeting on Sunday evenings because they wanted a church their friends would actually come to.

Why “Stillwater”

Named for a promise

Our name comes from Psalm 23, and from the river that runs slow and quiet past the old mill. Many of the people who first came to us were exhausted: shift workers, young parents and people carrying griefs they could not put down.

We wanted to be a place where a tired person could rest, and then, in time, be strengthened to go and care for someone else. That is still what we are trying to be.

What we’re about

Rest

We make room for people to stop, breathe and bring God what they are carrying.

Belong

We would rather know a few people well than have many people know of us.

Go

We leave on Sundays to love our neighbors, whether or not they ever visit us.

Along the way

Forty years, give or take

  1. 1987

    Twelve neighbors begin meeting in the mill workers’ hall.

  2. 1996

    We buy the old grain store on Mill Lane, and paint the doors red.

  3. 2008

    The Wednesday community supper begins, and has run every week since.

  4. 2016

    The pantry opens and now serves around 140 families a month.

  5. 2021

    We start streaming services, for those who cannot be in the room.

  6. Today

    Around 600 people call Stillwater home. Some of them started out at the very first supper.
