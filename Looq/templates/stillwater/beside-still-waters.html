Songs for Hard Days

Beside Still Waters

Rev. Hannah Okafor · November 1, 2026 · Psalm 23 · 34 min

Add your video or audio recording here.

“He leads me beside still waters. He restores my soul.”Psalm 23:2–3, World English Bible

Rest is not a reward for finishing everything. In Psalm 23 the shepherd makes the sheep lie down, long before the valley has been crossed.

Notes

  • The psalm opens with who God is, before saying anything about what we need.
  • Rest arrives before the dark valley, not after it.
  • A table is set in the presence of enemies. Peace does not wait for perfect conditions.

For your group

  1. Where in your week do you actually stop? What would have to change for that to happen?
  2. Which line of this psalm is hardest for you to say honestly right now?
  3. Who could you invite to rest with you this week?
