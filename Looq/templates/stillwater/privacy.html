The small print

Privacy Policy

Sample wording. Please have this reviewed for your own church before you publish it.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us, such as your name, email and message. If you register a child, we also ask for their name, age and care needs.

Prayer requests

Prayer requests are private by default and are read only by the prayer team. We only share a request on the prayer wall if you ask us to, and you can ask us to remove it at any time.

Children

We do not knowingly collect information directly from children. A parent or guardian must complete all forms relating to a child.

Giving

Gifts are processed by a secure payment provider. We do not store card details. We keep giving records as the law requires, and access is limited to our finance team.

Sharing

We never sell your information. We share it only with services that help us run the church, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time.
