Neighbors Food Drive

Help us stock the Ashford Falls pantry before the winter holidays.

Last year this drive filled the local pantry for six weeks. This year we are hoping for more, and we need hands for sorting, lifting and driving.

Canned vegetables, rice, cooking oil and diapers are the most needed items. Please check dates, and keep glass jars to a minimum.
