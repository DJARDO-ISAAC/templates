First time at a chop bar?

You're welcome here. A few things that help, especially if you're visiting Accra.

  1. 01

    Wash at the table

    We bring a bowl of water and soap before and after you eat.

  2. 02

    Eat with your right hand

    Fufu, banku and kenkey are traditionally eaten by hand. Cutlery is always available.

  3. 03

    Don't chew the fufu

    Pinch a small piece, dip it in the soup and swallow.

  4. 04

    Ask for medium pepper

    Our hot is properly hot. You can add fresh pepper at the table.

  5. 05

    Pay after you eat

    At the counter, by MoMo, card or cash.

  6. 06

    Say "medaase"

    Thank you in Twi. The cooks will appreciate it.
