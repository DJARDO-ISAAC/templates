Learning at the pot

Where it began

A table at Kaneshie

In {{ site.since }}, Maame Akua set up one table at Kaneshie market with a pot of light soup, a mortar and a pestle. Traders queued before noon, and by two o'clock the pot was empty.

Her daughter, {{ site.auntie }}, grew up pounding fufu after school. When Maame Akua retired, Adwoa carried the pot, the asanka and the recipes to Osu, and named the place for what it had always been: Abusua Pot, the family pot.

Today her children run the grill and the counter. The soups still cook on firewood from six every morning, because some things gas cannot do.
