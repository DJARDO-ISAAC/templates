The learning journey

From first letters to the BECE.

Ten years and one clear path. Scroll through the milestones every Nyansa Hill learner reaches.

  1. 01 KG 1 · age 4

    Play with purpose

    Four integrated themes (literacy, numeracy, creative arts, and Our World Our People) taught through play and song.

  2. 02 KG 2 · age 5

    Ready to read

    Letter sounds, counting and confident speaking in Twi, Ga and English, followed by a gentle readiness check before Basic 1.

  3. 03 Basic 1–3 · ages 6–8

    Two languages, one voice

    Children learn first in their Ghanaian language and move steadily to English, with daily reading blocks and hands-on maths.

  4. 04 Basic 4 · age 9

    English takes the lead

    English becomes the main language of teaching. National Standardised Test results help us spot gaps early.

  5. 05 Basic 6 · age 11

    Primary capstone

    A research project, a science fair entry and a smooth handover into the junior high programme.

  6. 06 JHS 1 · Basic 7

    The Common Core begins

    Computing, Career Technology, and Creative Arts & Design join the core subjects, alongside clubs and leadership roles.

  7. 07 JHS 2 · Basic 8

    Explore & aim

    Career guidance, termly mock exams and one-to-one target setting with a personal tutor.

  8. 08 JHS 3 · Basic 9

    BECE & beyond

    The WAEC BECE in May, then guided school selection on the CSSPS portal once results are released.
