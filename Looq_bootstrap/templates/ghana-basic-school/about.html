Welcome from the headteacher

“Akwaaba. You are welcome here.”

Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions a family makes. At Nyansa Hill we take that trust seriously. Our classes are small enough for every teacher to know each child: what excites them, what worries them and what they need next.

We teach the national curriculum, and we teach it well. We also believe a Ghanaian child should be fluent in their own language and proud of their heritage, as well as confident in English, French and technology.

The best way to understand our school is to see it on a normal day. I'd be delighted to show you around.

Our story

From one classroom to a community.

What began as a small kindergarten in a family home is now a full basic school, from KG 1 to JHS 3.

  1. 2008Founded as a kindergartenTwelve children and two teachers in East Legon.
  2. 2012Primary school opensBasic 1 to 6 move to our Spintex Road campus.
  3. 2016Junior High School beginsOur first JHS 3 cohort sat the BECE in 2019.
  4. 2021Common Core ProgrammeA new computing lab and Career Technology workshop for JHS.
  5. 2026Library & science blockMore than 6,000 books and two fully equipped labs.
Our people
