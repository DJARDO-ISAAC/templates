Ages 4–5
Foundation

Kindergarten · KG 1–2

Children learn through play, stories and song in four integrated themes: Language & Literacy, Numeracy, Creative Arts, and Our World Our People. Twi or Ga is used alongside English so every child feels at home.

  • At most 18 children per room, with a teacher and a teaching assistant
  • Phonics, counting and fine-motor work every day
  • A gentle readiness check in KG 2 before Basic 1
Ages 6–8
Lower Primary

Basic 1–3 · Primary 1–3

Strong foundations in reading, writing and number. Children learn first in their Ghanaian language and move steadily to English, following the national language policy.

  • A daily 40-minute reading block in English and a Ghanaian language
  • Hands-on maths with real objects, money and measures
  • French introduced through songs and games
Ages 9–11
Upper Primary

Basic 4–6 · Primary 4–6

English becomes the main language of teaching. Learners deepen science, computing and history, and take on projects that build research and presentation skills.

  • A weekly science lab session and computing lesson
  • National Standardised Test results used to spot gaps early
  • A Basic 6 capstone project and science fair
Ages 12–14
Junior High

JHS 1–3 · Basic 7–9

The Common Core Programme adds Computing, Career Technology, and Creative Arts & Design to the core subjects. A clear plan leads to the BECE and the move to senior high school.

  • Career guidance and a personal tutor for every learner
  • Full BECE mock exams each term from Basic 8
  • Step-by-step help with CSSPS school selection after results

Assessment & the BECE

No surprises, only progress.

We assess to help children learn, not to label them. Parents get a written terminal report and a conversation with the class teacher every term.

Continuous assessment

Class exercises, projects and practical work, recorded through the term.

End-of-term exams

From Basic 1, every subject. Results go into the terminal report.

National Standardised Test

Set by the Ministry of Education to check reading and maths at key stages of primary.

How the BECE is graded

WAEC grades each subject from 1 (highest) to 9 (lowest). For senior high school placement, the aggregate adds your grades in the four core subjects and your best two other subjects, so 6 is the best possible score.

BECE aggregate calculation
Part of the aggregateSubjectsBest grade
Core subjectsEnglish, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies4 × 1
Best two otherse.g. Computing, Career Technology, RME, Ghanaian Language, French, Creative Arts & Design2 × 1
Best possible aggregate6
