How admission works

  1. 1
    Apply online

    Complete the form below. It takes about five minutes.

  2. 2
    Visit

    Tour the school with your child and meet the phase lead.

  3. 3
    Assessment

    A play visit for KG, or a friendly literacy and numeracy check from Basic 1.

  4. 4
    Offer & welcome

    Get your offer letter, pay the acceptance fee, then receive the uniform and book lists.
