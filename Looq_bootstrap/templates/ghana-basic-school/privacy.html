1. Who we are

{{ site.name }} ("the school", "we") is a basic school at {{ site.address }} ({{ site.gesReg }}). We are responsible for the personal information we hold about pupils, parents, guardians, staff and visitors. We process it in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

2. What we collect

  • Pupils: name, date of birth, gender, class, previous school, reports and assessment results, attendance, and health or learning needs you tell us about.
  • Parents and guardians: name, relationship to the child, phone and WhatsApp numbers, email, area of residence, and payment records.
  • Website visitors: what you send us through our forms (tour requests, applications, enquiries and newsletter sign-ups).

3. How we use it

We use personal information to process admissions, teach and care for pupils, communicate with families, manage fees, keep the school safe, and meet our duties to the Ghana Education Service. We only send the newsletter to people who sign up, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell personal information. We share it only when needed:

  • with the Ghana Education Service and the Ministry of Education for official returns and national assessments;
  • with WAEC to register JHS 3 candidates for the BECE;
  • with health professionals in an emergency; and
  • with service providers who help us run the school, such as our form and email providers, under agreements that protect your data.

5. How long we keep it

We keep pupil records while your child is at the school and for a set period afterwards, as required for official records. Enquiries from families who do not enrol are deleted after 12 months.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you or your child, ask us to correct it, or object to some uses of it. To make a request, contact us using the details below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission, Ghana.

7. This website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Some content is loaded from other services, such as photos, the map on our contact page and the provider that delivers our forms. These services may receive your device's IP address when the content loads.
