A parent's guide to SHS selection after the BECE

Mr Kwame BoatengHead of JHS · BECE lead

The weeks after the BECE can feel stressful. With a little planning, choosing a senior high school becomes a clear, calm decision you make together with your child.

1. Understand the aggregate

WAEC grades each BECE subject from 1 (highest) to 9 (lowest). For placement, your child's aggregate is the total of:

  • the four core subjects: English Language, Mathematics, Science and Social Studies, and
  • their best two other subjects.

So the best possible aggregate is 6. A lower aggregate is better. Raw scores also matter when many candidates have the same aggregate, so every mark counts.

Tip: work out a realistic aggregate range from your child's mock results before the real results come out. It makes choosing schools much easier.

2. Know how CSSPS works

Placement into public senior high and TVET schools is done through the Computerised School Selection and Placement System (CSSPS) at cssps.gov.gh. Under the current process, candidates choose their schools after BECE results are released, during a selection window announced by GES.

  1. Wait for WAEC to release results, then check your child's grades.
  2. Log in to the CSSPS portal with your child's BECE index number when the selection window opens.
  3. Choose schools and programmes in order of preference, then submit before the deadline.
  4. Check the placement results on the same portal and follow the school's reporting instructions.

3. Build a balanced list

Choose a mix of schools: a couple of ambitious choices, several that match your child's expected aggregate, and at least one safe option. For each school, think about:

  • Programme: General Science, General Arts, Business, Visual Arts, Home Economics, Agricultural Science or a technical programme.
  • Day or boarding: distance, transport costs and your child's readiness to live away from home.
  • Fit: clubs, sports, faith background and the support the school offers.

4. How we help

Every JHS 3 family at Nyansa Hill gets a one-to-one school-selection meeting once results are out. We bring the mock and BECE results, talk through options and sit with you while you complete the CSSPS form if you'd like.

Please note: dates and steps can change each year. Always follow the latest official instructions from the Ghana Education Service and the CSSPS portal.
